Transmission: Why the 4EAT P2V1
Most people assume the 5MT is better. They are wrong for this application. The 4EAT P2V1 handles approximately 500 HP and splits torque 45/55 front/rear, similar to the STI manual. See xcceleration.com/4EAT-101.htm.

How to Drive It

  • Redline shifts under boost: stamp the throttle in one quick firm motion
  • Drive with left-foot braking
  • Launch at 3,600 RPM for maximum torque converter lockup
  • Daily: D range for early upshifts and fuel economy
  • Spirited/corners: 2nd range, then manually shift to 3rd

ECU Reset and Transmission Training

  1. Unplug the negative battery cable.
  2. Press the brake pedal three times to discharge capacitors.
  3. Reconnect the negative cable.
  4. Start the car. Drive in 1st gear, revving to redline for approximately 5 minutes.
  5. Drive aggressively through all ranges. Resets adaptive ECU learning and trains the transmission for this tune and valve body.
